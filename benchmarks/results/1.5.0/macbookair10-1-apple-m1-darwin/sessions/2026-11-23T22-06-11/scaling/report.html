Konjugate benchmark report

Konjugate 1.5.0 · engine 0.2.0 · commit 212d40f (with uncommitted changes) · --full · 2026-11-23T22:19:34.236Z

Konjugate is evaluated against established numerical and engineering benchmarks, with reproducible reference comparisons, conservation checks and performance measurements. Each problem below is run headless by the native engine from a committed model and compared with an analytic solution or an independent high-accuracy numerical reference. Konjugate integrates with fixed-step explicit or semi-implicit Euler node by node, both first order, or with Runge–Kutta 4 applied to the whole model at once, fourth order and as accurate across the coupling between nodes as within them; the results show where each is accurate and where it is not. How the numbers are made, and how to reproduce them, is in benchmarks/README.md.

Timing and memory

Measured on this computer only: after 1 warm-up run, 7 timed runs of each case, one at a time, under caffeinate. The headline is each case's minimum, since noise only adds time; the median, interquartile range and the minimum over the median say how steady the runs were. Wall time is the whole engine process (start, reading the model, integrating, writing the result); engine compute is the integration alone, as the engine reports it. Peak memory is the engine process's maximum resident set size.
