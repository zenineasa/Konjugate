Performance

Konjugate evaluated against established tools, reproducibly: the same equations, absolute tolerances and output samples under Konjugate's engine and its exported C++, SciPy's solve_ivp, Julia's DifferentialEquations.jl, at relative tolerances from 1e-3 to 1e-10. Each point is one tolerance: the error it reached against the minimum of its timed solves (after a warm-up), on this computer only; the minimum is the headline for every tool, since noise on a computer only adds time, and each table gives the median, interquartile range and minimum over median beside it, the spread between sessions when several were combined, and flags a measurement whose runs spread more than 5 % (interquartile range over median). Solve time is the integration alone: for Konjugate the engine's own integration time, for the exported C++ the whole program's run (it has no timer of its own, so its few milliseconds of starting and writing its CSV are included), for SciPy and Julia the call to the solver; starting, loading and compiling are in the table below. Konjugate, its C++ export, SciPy and Julia were timed natively in one session, interleaved: each round ran every tool's configurations in turn, once untimed and once timed, and the rounds repeated, with Docker quit. The scripts are in benchmarks/comparisons/; the method is in benchmarks/README.md.
