Konjugate is an open-source graph-native simulation engine for building composable engineering simulations and digital twins.

Build models from connected components and relationships, then validate and execute them with the native simulation engine.

By treating engineering models as dynamic graphs, Konjugate allows you to assemble complex systems from modular components and relationships. Define your logic, validate the topology and execute high-fidelity simulations; all within a unified, canvas-first environment.
